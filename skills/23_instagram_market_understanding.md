# Instagram market understanding **[algorithms and features change often; verify with Instagram's own help and your insights]**

## What Instagram rewards (as commonly observed, not guaranteed)
- Reels that hold attention (watch time, completion, rewatches), plus shares and saves; sends via DM are widely reported as a strong signal.
- Original content; watermarked reposts from other apps tend to be deprioritised.
- Consistency and clear niche: the account is classified by what it posts repeatedly.

## Audience for BeamNG content
Car-culture fans (not only gamers), clip/meme consumers, drift/rally fans, builders. Many don't play BeamNG: the hook must make sense without game knowledge ("watch this car survive...").

## Content pillars (mix per week)
1. Showcase (cinematic mod clip)
2. Crash/chaos (high share rate)
3. Education (tips, how it's made, lessons)
4. Community (user clips, polls)
5. Behind the scenes / dev progress
Ratio idea: 2 showcase, 2 crash, 1 education, 1 community per week.

## Profile
Name field with keyword ("BeamNG Mods | <brand>"), bio: who/what/benefit/CTA, link in bio (hub page), highlights (Download, Mods, FAQ), consistent avatar and cover style.

## Reels craft
9:16, hook in 1 s, captions on screen, 7-30 s, loopable end, original audio or licensed track, cover frame designed (grid looks consistent).

## Captions & hashtags
First line hooks; keep 3-5 relevant hashtags; ask a question to prompt comments; avoid banned/spammy tags **[verify current guidance]**.

## Rhythm & timing
4-5 Reels/week for 4 weeks; stories daily (polls, behind-scenes); check Insights > Followers > Most active times, then post 30-60 min before peak.

## Growth levers
- Collab posts with other creators (both audiences).
- Reply to comments within the first hour; reply with videos.
- Trend jumping: use a trending format only if it fits your niche; adapt within 48 h.
- Cross-promote on stories, YouTube, TikTok without stripping watermarks wrongly.

## Metrics
Reach, plays, 3-s hold, average watch time, shares, saves, profile visits, follows per Reel, link taps. Follows-per-reach is the key audience-building ratio.

## Risks
Copyrighted music takedowns, sudden reach drops, inactive/fake followers (never buy them), changes to link rules.
