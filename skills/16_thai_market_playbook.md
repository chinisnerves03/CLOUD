# Thai market playbook **[audience habits are general knowledge; confirm with your own analytics]**

## Why bilingual
English reaches the global audience; Thai content wins a loyal local niche with less competition. Publish both: English on the repository page (search), Thai on social posts (reach).

## Platforms
- **Facebook** (Pages, Groups, Reels): still large in Thailand; car-culture and game groups are active. Join relevant groups and read rules first.
- **TikTok**: strong for short vertical clips; Thai captions and Thai trending sounds (licensed).
- **YouTube**: Thai gaming/tuner audience; Thai titles with English keywords ("BeamNG ม็อดรถ ...").
- **Instagram Reels, X/Twitter, Lemon8, Discord/LINE OpenChat** for communities **[verify current popularity]**.
- Pantip and Thai game forums for threads and feedback.

## Content hooks with local flavour
- Thai roads and street scenes, taxi/tuk-tuk/songthaew/pickup/Hilux-style vehicles, tuned Honda/Toyota/Nissan culture, Thai mountain passes, night street vibes.
- Humour and relatable situations; short punchy Thai text overlays.
- "รถไทยในเกม" (Thai cars in the game) recreations, with respect for brand rules.

## Language
- Casual Thai, short sentences, emoji sparingly, key English terms kept (BeamNG, mod, drift).
- Bilingual subtitles: Thai primary, English secondary on long videos.
- Keep Thai wording consistent for the name of your mod series.

## Timing
Test evening 18:00–22:00 and lunch 11:30–13:00 (Bangkok time) first, then follow your analytics.

## Community
- Thai-language Discord channel or LINE OpenChat for support.
- Collaborate with Thai YouTubers/streamers: give them early access.

## Monetisation notes
Thai-language Patreon alternatives and local payment methods (PromptPay QR) lower friction for local supporters **[verify availability and fees]**.
