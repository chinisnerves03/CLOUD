# Positioning & audience

## Principle
Popularity is mostly decided **before** you build: a clear idea that fits an existing audience's desire, with a visual hook that works in a 3-second clip.

## Research (1–2 days, free)
- Browse the top/trending mods on the repository: what categories dominate, what's missing, what comments ask for ("please make a …").
- Search YouTube/TikTok for "BeamNG" + category; note views vs channel size to find *over-performing* clips (outliers).
- Read r/BeamNG, the official forum, and Discord requests for repeated asks.
- Check Google Trends / YouTube search suggest for car names and map types.
- Tools like vidIQ-style keyword tools can estimate search volume **[verify]**.

## Choosing the idea — score 1–5 each
| Criterion | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| Demand | do people already ask for/search this? |
| Gap | is existing supply poor or missing? |
| Clip-ability | does it make a great 10-second video (drift, jump, crash, rally)? |
| Feasibility | can you finish it in the time you have? |
| Edge | what do you do better than anyone (accuracy, sound, handling, theme)? |
Build only ideas scoring high on clip-ability and demand.

## Differentiation levers
Authentic handling, great sound, many configs, damage detail, theme (JDM, Thai street, rally, offroad, truck, bus), unique map with a purpose, quality of presentation.

## One-sentence pitch test
"**[Mod name] is a [type] for people who like [activity], with [unique hook].**" If you cannot write it, do not start.

## Naming & branding
- Short, searchable, spellable, available on the repo and as a handle. Include a descriptive keyword in the title ("— Touge Map", "— Drift Build").
- Consistent look: logo/watermark, colour palette, thumbnail style, so every post is recognisably yours.

## Audience segments
Casual crash-fans, drifters/racers, realism/sim fans, builders/tuners, map explorers, YouTubers needing content. Create at least one hook per segment.

## Series thinking
Release in a **series** (mod #1, #2, #3 with the same style). Each release feeds the next one's audience.

## Making a paid mod worth more than its price (TurboCraft, $5 critique)
Buyers compare a paid mod with free repository mods. Pay is justified only by what free mods do not give.
1. **Visible wow in 5 seconds**: tip fireball + light on bumper/road, colour by gear (orange/white/blue). Show it in the first frame of every clip.
2. **Real sound, not synthesized**: CC0 recordings for pops, bangs and the drift limiter; earshot `match` should read "strong" before shipping.
3. **Universal = the real selling point**: works on many cars without per-car setup. Publish a tested-cars list (car, engine, result) and keep it growing.
4. **Control**: in-game sliders/presets (street / race / drift / anti-lag), live monitor app, per-car profiles saved.
5. **Proof, not claims**: side-by-side clip stock vs TurboCraft, same car, same camera, same throttle input; numbers from the monitor (boost, rpm).
6. **Trust**: changelog, version, licences/credits file, clean uninstall, no broken saves, fast bug replies.
7. **Ongoing value**: roadmap + regular updates included in the price (buyers pay for the future, not one file).
8. **Free tier**: a free "lite" (sound or flames only) on the repository; paid version adds the rest. Critics then see the free version is already good. Check BeamNG repository rules for lite/paid splits [verify].
9. **Price anchor**: show what $5 buys vs separate mods (backfire + sound + turbo + monitor).
Avoid: anything copied from other mods, BY-NC sounds, claims not tested in game.
