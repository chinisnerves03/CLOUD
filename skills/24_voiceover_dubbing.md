# Voice-over

## Script for the ear
- Short sentences, 12-16 words max; one idea each; read aloud before recording.
- Pace: about 2.5-3 words/second for energetic shorts; 2-2.5 for narration. 30 s ~ 70-85 words.
- Hook line first, payoff line last; mark breaths and emphasis with / and CAPS.
- Thai: write how people speak, not formal register; keep English product terms; check tone particles (ครับ/ค่ะ/นะ) match the brand voice consistently.

## Recording (human)
- Quiet room with soft furnishings, mic 10-15 cm off-axis with pop filter, gain so peaks hit -12 dB; record 10 s room tone; do 3 takes per line; slate each take.
- Direct performance: smile on friendly lines, lower and slower for drama, vary pitch to avoid monotone.

## AI voices **[tools and policy change; verify]**
- Choose a voice that fits the character/brand; keep one voice per role across the series.
- Audio tags/direction in the text help delivery on models that support them; the line text must match the script.
- One take per line when credits matter; listen to every line before mixing; regenerate only on real defects.
- Never clone a real person's voice without consent. Disclose AI voices where platform rules or fairness require (e.g. in video descriptions) **[verify]**.
- Cost: usually per character; estimate before generating and get approval.

## Syncing to picture
- Cut picture to the voice (not the reverse); keep 0.2-0.45 s gaps between lines; trim silence; use subtitles from the same script (SRT).
- Dubbing existing videos: translate for meaning and length, not word-for-word; leave visual cues intact; lip-sync is not needed for gameplay narration.

## Clean-up & mix
- High-pass 80 Hz, gentle de-noise, de-ess, light compression (2:1-3:1), EQ presence 3-5 kHz.
- Voice at about -16 to -12 LUFS before music; music ducked 8-12 dB under voice; final mix near -14 LUFS **[verify target]**.
- Add small reverb only to blend with SFX.

## Checklist
- [ ] Script read aloud, timing fits the cut
- [ ] No clipping, breaths natural
- [ ] Every AI line listened to
- [ ] Subtitles match audio
- [ ] Disclosure added if AI voice used
