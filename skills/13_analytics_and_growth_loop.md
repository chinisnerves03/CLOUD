# Analytics & growth loop

## The funnel
`Impressions → Views → Retention → Profile/Page visits → Downloads/Subscriptions → Ratings/Comments → Shares`
Find the biggest drop and fix that first.

## Metrics that matter
| Stage | Metric | Rough read **[benchmarks vary; compare to your own history]** |
|-------|--------|------|
| Reach | impressions, unique viewers | trend week over week |
| Hook | 3-second view rate / first 30 s retention | <60% on shorts = weak hook |
| Interest | average view duration, % viewed | higher % viewed → more distribution |
| Click | CTR on thumbnail/title | ~4–10% typical range on YouTube; below 3% rework |
| Convert | page visits per 1000 views, downloads per 1000 views | the true mod KPI |
| Retain | comments, shares, returning viewers | quality of audience |

## Tracking downloads
- Official repo shows downloads/subscriptions per mod; log them daily in a sheet.
- Use unique short links per platform (UTM parameters, link shorteners with stats) to attribute traffic **[verify the repo/pages allow it]**.
- Record every post: date, platform, format, hook, length, result, in one table.

## Weekly review (30 min)
1. Export last 7 days per platform.
2. Rank posts by views and by downloads per 1000 views.
3. Write: what is the pattern in the top 3 and bottom 3? (hook type, car, length, time, thumbnail).
4. Decide **one change** per variable for next week (A/B: change one thing only).
5. Update the content calendar.

## Experiments to run
- Hook variants (question vs shock vs statement).
- Thumbnail variants (with text vs without).
- Posting time (same days, two time slots, 3 weeks).
- Video length (15 s vs 30 s).
- Collab/outreach vs organic.

## Decision rules
- If a format gets 2× your median views twice, make three more in that format.
- If views are high but downloads low → mod page/CTA problem, not content problem.
- If downloads are high and ratings low → quality/bugs problem: patch before promoting.
- Stop a series after 5 posts with no improvement; change angle.

## Do not
- Buy views/followers or use bot services (policy violation, and kills the signal quality).
- Judge a post before 48–72 h (long tails).
