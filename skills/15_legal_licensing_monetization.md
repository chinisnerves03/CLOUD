# Legal & money — **not legal advice; check the repository's current rules and local law [verify]**

## Content you can and cannot use
- **Your own work**: fine. Keep source files and dated backups as proof.
- **Third-party models/textures/sounds/music**: only with a licence that allows redistribution in mods (check CC variants: CC0 ok; BY needs credit; NC bans commercial use; ND may block adaptation).
- **Ripped assets from other games**: don't. It gets mods removed and accounts banned.
- **Real cars / brands**: depicting real vehicles is common practice in the community but logos/names are trademarks; repositories often have rules about branding. Read the repo guidelines and consider generic naming where required.
- **Music in videos**: use licensed or royalty-free tracks; keep licence proof.
- **Map data (real world)**: verify the data licence (e.g. open government data vs restricted).

## Credits
Include a `CREDITS.txt` and a section on the release page. Re-credit after updates.

## Ways to earn (ranked by friction)
1. **Donations/tips** (Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, PayPal.me) — ask politely on page and video; no paywall.
2. **Memberships/Patreon**: early access, voting on next mod, supporter role, behind-the-scenes. Rules on selling paid mods vs early access differ **[verify repo policy; many keep final mods free]**.
3. **Ad revenue** from video platforms (needs eligibility thresholds **[verify current]**).
4. **Sponsorships** (hardware, tools, gear) — disclose clearly (#ad), keep editorial independence.
5. **Services**: commissions for custom skins/configs, tutorials, courses, asset packs you own the rights to.
6. **Sell tools you built** (kits/templates) — not the game's assets.

## Ethics
- Don't paywall bug fixes.
- Don't make misleading "free download" claims on pages that hide fees.
- Disclose AI-generated voices or visuals where platform rules or common courtesy demand it.
- Don't attach malware, trackers, or ad-installers to downloads.

## Taxes & registration
Income from donations and ads may be taxable; keep records and consult a local professional.
