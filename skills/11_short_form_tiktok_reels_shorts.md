# Short-form (9:16)

Platform limits and algorithm behaviour change often **[verify before relying]**.

## Formats that tend to perform for car-game mods
- **Satisfying drift/touge loop** (looping end into start).
- **"Rate this crash 1–10"** slow-mo with on-screen score.
- **Before/after or stock vs modded** side-by-side.
- **"Can it survive X?"** challenge (jump, cliff, train).
- **Build in 15 s** (parts snap onto the car).
- **Hidden detail** ("watch the suspension flex").
- **POV cockpit** at night with rain and neon.
- **Dev diary** (tiny update, community name-in-credits).

## Structure (15–35 s)
- **0–1 s**: motion + text hook, visible without sound ("This car has a secret").
- **1–5 s**: promise payoff shown fast.
- Middle: escalation, one idea only.
- End: payoff or twist, then a loop point or one-line CTA ("Link in bio").
- Avoid a long logo intro; branding goes in a corner overlay.

## Technical
- 1080×1920, 30/60 fps, safe zone: keep text out of the bottom ~20% and right edge (UI overlays).
- Always burn in subtitles (most watch muted); big, high contrast, 1–2 lines.
- Use platform music only if licence allows commercial/creator use; otherwise in-video music you have rights to.
- Native upload (no watermarks from other apps).

## Cadence
Consistency beats volume: e.g. 3–5 posts per week for 4 weeks, same time slot, then review analytics (see `analytics_and_growth_loop`).

## Captions & tags
- First line = hook sentence; second line = context + CTA; 3–5 relevant tags (#beamng #beamngdrive #carmods + niche tag). Hashtag spam does not help **[verify]**.
- Pin a comment with the download path (never put raw links where blocked; use link in bio or the platform's allowed method).
- Reply to comments within the first hour with a video reply if possible.

## Reuse
One shoot → 1 long video + 5–10 shorts. Re-edit; do not repost identical clips on the same platform.
