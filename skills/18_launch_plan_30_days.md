# 30-day launch plan (adjust to your capacity)

## Days -14 to -1 — Pre-launch
- D-14: pitch sentence, name, logo, colour palette; create channels/handles; Discord set up.
- D-12: record first b-roll (golden hour hero shots, one crash, one drive).
- D-10: teaser #1 (3 s silhouette/detail + "coming soon"); recruit 5–10 testers.
- D-7: teaser #2 (one feature); collect tester bug reports; fix.
- D-5: trailer edit; thumbnail drafts; write release page.
- D-3: final zip test on clean profile; set up tracking sheet and unique links.
- D-1: schedule posts; brief creators with press kit (link, facts, clips).

## Day 0 — Launch
- Publish mod page; upload trailer; post short-form clips on all platforms; post in communities per their rules.
- Reply to all comments for 24 h; log first-day metrics.

## Days 1–7
- Daily short (different hook each day), 1 longer showcase; share tester clips.
- Collect bugs; patch v1.0.1 by day 5–7 and announce it.

## Days 8–14
- Format that over-performed → make 3 variants.
- Outreach round 2 to creators who showed interest.
- Challenge/community event ("best screenshot wins a feature").

## Days 15–21
- Release a new config or update (v1.1) with a mini trailer.
- Second long video: "how I made it" / dev diary (builds trust and search traffic).

## Days 22–30
- Review analytics (see `analytics_and_growth_loop`): which platform, format, hook, time.
- Decide next mod using demand + clip-ability scores (`positioning_and_audience`).
- Publish retrospective; thank the community; open a poll for the next project.

## Cadence after launch
2–3 posts/week, 1 long video every 2 weeks, 1 patch/month, 1 new mod every 6–10 weeks.

## KPIs to set before D0 (examples, adjust to your level)
- Downloads/subscriptions week 1 and week 4
- Average rating
- Subscribers/followers gained per platform
- Median short-form views and best post
- Bug reports resolved within 7 days
