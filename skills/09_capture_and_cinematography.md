# Capture & cinematography

## Technical
- Record with OBS Studio (free). 1920×1080 or 2560×1440, 60 fps, bitrate ~20–40 Mbps CBR/CQP, hardware encoder (NVENC/AMF/QuickSync). Record to MKV, remux to MP4 after.
- Capture the game window/game capture, not the desktop. Hide UI/HUD for cinematic shots.
- Lock the game to a stable frame rate; stutter ruins slow motion. Lower sim load (fewer traffic cars) for beauty shots.
- Record at 60 fps even for 30 fps output; slow-motion needs spare frames.
- Use the in-game replay/free camera (when available) to re-record the same event from several angles **[verify features]**.

## Look
- Time of day: golden hour (low sun) and dusk flatter cars; midday flat light looks cheap.
- Reflective surface: wet road adds depth. Clean sky/weather.
- Depth of field and motion blur tuned lightly; field of view 35–50° for beauty, wider for speed.
- Clean the scene: no random debris, no AI cars in the wrong place, tidy shadows.

## Shots every showcase needs
1. Establishing wide: car in environment.
2. Hero 3/4 front low, slowly pushing in.
3. Details: wheels, lights, interior, badge (macro).
4. Driving: chase cam, hood cam, roadside pan, drone/top-down.
5. Action: drift, jump, cornering, offroad.
6. Damage: crash in slow motion from 2 angles (clips that go viral).
7. Config comparison / customization.
8. End frame with name, download hint, and a logo.

## Direction
- Plan each shot: subject, camera, motion, duration (2–4 s).
- Do multiple takes; capture 3× footage versus final length.
- Use a repeatable "test track" and "test crash location" for consistency.
- Keep a shot log to speed up editing (see `video_editing_trailers`).
