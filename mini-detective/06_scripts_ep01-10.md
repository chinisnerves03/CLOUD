# Scripts — Entries 1–10

Format: each line is `SPEAKER: [audio tags] text` for ElevenLabs v3. `VISUAL` and `SFX` lines are for the animator and editor and are never sent to the voice generator.
Target: 30–45 seconds, about 90–110 spoken words.
Entry end card (every episode): cream paper, navy text, small keyhole eye: "Entry [N+1] tomorrow."

---

## ENTRY 1 — The Button That Wasn't There

VISUAL (0–3s): Black. A thin line of dawn light slides across Button Square.
NARRATOR: [softly] Lost and Found Log, entry one. [pause] In a city built from things people lost… something just went missing.

VISUAL (3–9s): Wide shot of the plaza. A round empty plinth in the middle. Crowd murmuring.
NARRATOR: The Great Red Button. Wide as a fountain. The pride of Pocket City. Gone before breakfast.

VISUAL (9–16s): Pip yawns, walks into frame, thimble crooked.
PIP: [curious] Huh. That's… interesting.
JUNO: [sarcastic] Interesting? Pip, somebody walked off with a whole BUTTON.

VISUAL (16–26s): Pip kneels. No marks. Magnifier slides in.
NARRATOR: Detective Pip Thimble checked the stone. No drag marks. No footprints. [short pause] Just one thing, scratched very small.

VISUAL (26–30s): CLUE CARD: an eye shaped like a keyhole. SFX: paper tear.
PIP: [whispers] Small clue. Big picture.

VISUAL (30–40s): Camera tilts up. A thin thread disappears into the dark. It twitches.
NARRATOR: [slowly] And far above the plaza… a thread tightened. [long pause] Something was still pulling.

END CARD: "Entry two tomorrow."

SFX: dawn birds, crowd murmur, paper tear, soft three-note tick at the thread twitch.

## ENTRY 2 — Footprints on the Ceiling

VISUAL: Pip and Juno at the foot of the plaza wall, looking up.
NARRATOR: [softly] Lost and Found Log, entry two. [short pause] The thief left footprints. [dramatically] On the ceiling.

VISUAL: Juno's glider swoops. Dusty prints walk across the underside of a shelf.
JUNO: [excited] Nobody asked, but people don't walk on ceilings.
PIP: [curious] Somebody did.

VISUAL: Brass stomps in, mustache bristling.
BRASS: [shouting] Drift accident! Buttons fall UP sometimes! Case closed!
PIP: [calmly] Inspector… buttons don't fall up.

VISUAL: Pip lifts a gleaming strand with tweezers.
NARRATOR: Pip found a single thread. Silk, fine as breath, flecked with gold.
PIP: [whispers] Nobody in Pocket City makes thread like this.

VISUAL: Brass pats his coat. Pat. Pat. Panic.
BRASS: [nervous] My… badge?

VISUAL: Brass's desk. A clean rectangle in the dust where the badge lay. Beside it, a scratched keyhole eye.
NARRATOR: [slowly] There was only one thing left behind. [long pause] An eye, watching.

END CARD: "Entry three tomorrow."

SFX: glider whoosh, thread shimmer, dust puff.

## ENTRY 3 — Madame Velvet's Silent Night

VISUAL: The Tin Lounge. A spotlight on an empty stand.
NARRATOR: [softly] Lost and Found Log, entry three. [pause] The best singer in Pocket City just stopped, mid-note.

VISUAL: Velvet on stage, hands empty. The audience stares.
VELVET: [softly] It's nothing, darlings. Nothing at all.

VISUAL: Fuzz drifts to Pip's table, shedding fluff.
FUZZ: [whispers] That'll cost you a crumb.
PIP: [curious] One crumb.
FUZZ: [mischievously] Letter. Last week. Sealed with an eye.

VISUAL: Close on a wax seal: the keyhole eye.
NARRATOR: Velvet knew the symbol. She just hoped the symbol did not know her.

VISUAL: Backstage, Velvet's glove trembles.
VELVET: [softly] They call themselves the Keyhole Society. [pause] They take what is lost… and what is loved.

VISUAL: She hums three slow notes. Pip stops mid-step.
PIP: [whispers] I know that song. [short pause] Why do I know that song?

END CARD: "Entry four tomorrow."

SFX: soft piano, crowd murmur dying, three humming notes.

## ENTRY 4 — The Sock Marsh

VISUAL: Fog. Hills of mismatched socks.
NARRATOR: [softly] Lost and Found Log, entry four. [short pause] Nothing survives the Sock Marsh. Except the socks.

VISUAL: Pip steps on a coin; it sinks an inch.
PIP: [nervous] This is fine. This is… squishy.
JUNO: [sarcastic] Squishy? I've lost a glider and my dignity.

VISUAL: Juno's glider stuck in a striped sock. Pip tugs her free.
NARRATOR: They followed coins like stepping stones, deeper into the wool.

VISUAL: A shape in the fog: a warehouse made of shoeboxes.
PIP: [whispers] Juno. Look.

VISUAL: Rows of shelves. Everything sorted by color. Each tagged with a keyhole eye. On a ledger: "Collection 17 of 400."
NARRATOR: Seventeen. Out of four hundred.
JUNO: [nervous] Pip… the Red Button is right there.

VISUAL: Every light snaps out. A door slams from outside.
NARRATOR: [slowly] Somebody knew they were here. [long pause] And somebody had the key.

END CARD: "Entry five tomorrow."

SFX: wool squelch, fog hum, door slam, lock click.

## ENTRY 5 — 3:11

VISUAL: Close-up: a pocket watch ticking. 3:11.
NARRATOR: [softly] Lost and Found Log, entry five. [pause] Every theft happened at three-eleven in the morning.

VISUAL: Pip's wall of red thread connecting pins. Every pin says 3:11.
PIP: [curious] Three-eleven. Eleven minutes. Why eleven minutes?

VISUAL: Cog's workshop. Cog polishes a gear. The Great Clock behind him is 11 minutes slow.
COG: [nervous] The clock loses eleven minutes every night. Time waits for no one, Detective, but sometimes it leaves early.

VISUAL: Night. Pip on watch in the plaza. His eyes blink. A jump cut: the sky is slightly lighter.
NARRATOR: Pip stayed awake. He is sure he stayed awake.

VISUAL: His notebook. A fresh line in his own handwriting: "Don't wait up. — P."
PIP: [whispers] I didn't write that.

VISUAL: Dawn. Cog's door swings open. The workshop is empty. The Great Clock is stopped at 3:11.
NARRATOR: [slowly] The thief wasn't stealing things. [pause] The thief was stealing time.

END CARD: "Entry six tomorrow."

SFX: ticking, the three-note tick that ends in silence.

## ENTRY 6 — Locked Room, Tiny Room

VISUAL: A matchbox door. A bolt on the inside.
NARRATOR: [softly] Lost and Found Log, entry six. [short pause] A locked room. Inside a matchbox. Locked from the inside.

VISUAL: Juno kicks the door.
JUNO: [shouting] Nobody asked, but I'm breaking it!
PIP: [calmly] Wait. Look at the size of the keyhole.

VISUAL: Pip measures the keyhole with his thumb. Then the bolt. Then the pendulum slot.
NARRATOR: Pip stopped thinking like a detective. He started thinking like someone very, very small.

VISUAL: On the floor, tiny gears laid in a line.
PIP: [whispers] Gear code.
JUNO: [curious] What's it say?
PIP: [slowly] "BRASS. KNOWS."

VISUAL: Pip looks into the pendulum shaft: a rope ladder leads down.
NARRATOR: Professor Cog hadn't been taken. He'd left. On purpose. Down the pendulum shaft.

VISUAL: A creak. Pip turns. The Inspector stands in the doorway, a crumpled letter in his fist, trembling.
NARRATOR: [slowly] And the man who closed every case… finally opened his mouth.

END CARD: "Entry seven tomorrow."

SFX: door thud, pendulum swing, creak.

---

## ENTRY 7 — Inspector Brass Has a Secret

VISUAL: A bench on Skirting Board Street. Evening. Brass sits with his helmet in his hands.
NARRATOR: [softly] Lost and Found Log, entry seven. [pause] The man who closes every case… just opened his own.

VISUAL: Brass holds a small empty box.
BRASS: [tired] Forty years ago I lost my wife's ring. The Drift took it.

VISUAL: Close on the letter with a keyhole-eye seal.
BRASS: [softly] Then a letter came. "Look away, and it comes back."

VISUAL: Juno's mouth opens. She closes it.
JUNO: [softly] Nobody asked. But I'm sorry, Inspector.

VISUAL: Brass presses a brass key into Pip's hand: the Tube Key.
BRASS: If you want answers, ask the one who writes the letters.
NARRATOR: He wasn't the thief. He was a prisoner of something he'd lost.

VISUAL: Pip's Bureau. A brass speaking tube mouthpiece trembles on the wall.
NARRATOR: That night, the tube began to rattle. [pause] By itself.
COLLECTOR: [deep voice] [slowly] Hello, Detective.

END CARD: "Entry eight tomorrow."

SFX: evening crickets, key clink, tube rattle, echoing voice.

## ENTRY 8 — The Voice in the Tube

VISUAL: Pip at his desk, one hand on the tube.
NARRATOR: [softly] Lost and Found Log, entry eight. [short pause] Pip had one chance to ask the thief a question.

VISUAL: Pip leans close.
PIP: [nervously] Why are you taking them?
COLLECTOR: [deep voice] [slowly] Because they were never yours.

VISUAL: Juno whispers behind Pip: "Ask who he is."
PIP: Who are you?
COLLECTOR: [slowly] Someone who has been patient for a very long time.

VISUAL: A silhouette in the tube mouth: the Collector's hat.
COLLECTOR: Stop, Detective. And I will return everything you have ever lost. [long pause] Even your name.

VISUAL: Pip's hand shakes. He looks at his thimble in the lamplight.
NARRATOR: Pip had arrived in the Drift as a baby, wrapped in a handkerchief with an eye stitched in the corner.
COLLECTOR: [softly] I know who left you there.

VISUAL: Pip stands.
PIP: [determined] I'm going up there.
JUNO: [sarcastic] Nobody asked, but I'm coming.

END CARD: "Entry nine tomorrow."

SFX: rain on window, tube hum, a tiny clink as the thimble sets down.

## ENTRY 9 — The Night Delivery

VISUAL: The plaza at 3:10. Silence. Pip and Juno crouch behind a spool.
NARRATOR: [softly] Lost and Found Log, entry nine. [pause] At three-eleven, Pocket City looked up.

VISUAL: The thread lowers, hooks a clock face, lifts it into the dark.
JUNO: [whispers] That's the plaza clock.
PIP: [whispers] Hold on.

VISUAL: They grab the thread. Wind. The city shrinks below.
NARRATOR: They climbed. Hand over hand. Past the roofs. Past the shelf. Into the dark.

VISUAL: Juno's glider shreds in the wind.
JUNO: [shouting] I loved that glider!
PIP: [shouting] I'll buy you a new one!

VISUAL: A hatch, shaped like a keyhole eye. The latch is thimble-shaped.
PIP: [curious] It's… the size of my hat.

VISUAL: Pip lifts the thimble off his head. It clicks into the lock. Light spills out.
NARRATOR: And on the other side… was a place far bigger than the city.

END CARD: "Entry ten tomorrow."

SFX: wind, thread creak, glider tear, lock click, soft gold light.

---

## ENTRY 10 — The Keyhole (Season 1 finale)

VISUAL: A vast hall. Shelves rise out of frame. Pip and Juno are tiny dots on the floor.
NARRATOR: [softly] Lost and Found Log, entry ten. [pause] Pocket City was never the whole world.

VISUAL: Towers of keys, umbrellas, gloves, letters, a small child's shoe.
JUNO: [whispers] Everything anyone ever lost.
PIP: [whispers] Everything.

VISUAL: At the end of the hall, a door with a keyhole as tall as a building. Gold light from within.
COLLECTOR: [deep voice] [slowly] You are very close, Pip.

VISUAL: Pip walks to the keyhole. Juno holds back.
NARRATOR: Pip stepped up. Small clue. Big picture. [short pause] And then, the biggest picture of all.

VISUAL: Through the keyhole: an enormous eye. Mustard iris. A keyhole pupil.
VOICE (Collector, very low): [deep voice] [long pause] Hello… again… Pip.

VISUAL: The eye blinks. Every light in Pocket City goes out, one by one.
NARRATOR: [softly] Lost and Found Log. Entry ten. [long pause] End of season.

END CARD: "Season 1 ends. Entry 11 returns soon."

SFX: vast room tone, deep drone, a heartbeat-like thump, lights popping out.

## Word count and timing checklist
- Count only spoken words. If an entry runs over 110 words, cut a joke before cutting a clue.
- Read it aloud with a stopwatch. 30–45 seconds with pauses.
- The first line must work with the sound off: on-screen text "Entry N" and a clear image.
