# Characters

Everything here is original. Colors use the series palette:
kraft `#C89B6D`, navy `#1F2A44`, mustard `#E0A526`, tomato `#C8452F`, cream `#F3E9D2`, teal `#2F8F8B`.

All characters are 3 cm tall Thimblefolk unless stated.

---

## Pip Thimble — the detective
- **Role:** Hero. Private detective for Pocket City's Lost & Found Bureau.
- **Look:** Round head, messy ink-black hair poking out from under a silver thimble worn as a hat. Trench coat cut from an old map (kraft with faint blue lines). Magnifying lens made from a watch crystal on a thread cord. Red thread tie. Notebook made from a postage-stamp pad.
- **Shape language:** Round and soft. Big head, small body.
- **Personality:** Curious, observant, overthinks, polite to everyone, hates being called "cute". Gets quiet when he is close to the truth.
- **Voice:** Friendly young-adult American, warm. Short lines.
- **Catchphrase:** "Small clue. Big picture."
- **Secret:** The thimble is the only thing he arrived with. (See Knowledge Base.)
- **Image prompt:**
  ```

Front view neutral pose with separate arms and legs for rigging, plus five facial expressions (neutral, curious, surprised, worried, determined). Plain cream background, no text.

## Juno Quill — the courier
- **Role:** Pip's partner. Fastest flyer in Pocket City on a folded-paper glider named "Receipt".
- **Look:** Tall for a Thimblefolk, teal flight cap cut from a bottle cap liner, goggles made from two sequins, scarf from a ribbon end in tomato red, messenger bag made from an envelope.
- **Shape language:** Angular and sharp. Pointy elbows, a lean silhouette.
- **Personality:** Brave, impatient, sarcastic, loyal. Always right a second after Pip is wrong.
- **Voice:** Young London woman, quick, a bit Cockney.
- **Habit:** "Nobody asked, but…"

## Inspector Gaffer Brass — the police chief
- **Role:** Chief of the Pocket City Watch. Wants cases closed before lunch.
- **Look:** Short, wide, barrel-chested. Helmet made from a brass key head, badge cut from a coin, thick mustache made of a paintbrush bristle bundle. Navy uniform from a glove finger.
- **Shape language:** Square and heavy.
- **Personality:** Gruff, defensive, secretly tired and frightened. Protects the city by pretending nothing is wrong.
- **Voice:** American working-class baritone.
- **Habit:** "Case closed!"

## Fuzz — the dust-bunny gossip
- **Role:** Informant. Not a Thimblefolk: a dust bunny, bigger than Pip, shaped like a gray cloud with two button eyes.
- **Look:** Soft gray paper scraps layered into a puffy cloud, two black button eyes, tiny stitched smile, always shedding a little.
- **Personality:** Sells rumors for crumbs. Cowardly, cheerful, surprisingly reliable.
- **Voice:** Husky trickster.
- **Habit:** "That'll cost you a crumb."
- **Image prompt:**
  ```

## The Collector — the villain
- **Role:** Mastermind behind the thefts. Heard through speaking tubes, never seen in Season 1.
- **Look (shown only as silhouette):** Very tall thin outline, long coat, hat with a wide brim, one gloved hand holding a thread. In the shadow, a keyhole-shaped eye shines mustard-yellow.
- **Personality:** Calm, polite, patient. Never raises his voice. Speaks as if everyone is already late.
- **Voice:** Deep, raspy American.
- **Image prompt (silhouette card):**
  ```
  Paper cut-out collage silhouette of a very tall thin figure in a long coat and wide-brim hat, cut from black construction paper against a navy background, holding a single taut thread. Where the face should be, a mustard-yellow keyhole-shaped eye glows. Torn paper edges, paper grain, no gradients, vertical 9:16.
  ```

## The Giant Eye — Season 1 finale only
- **Look:** A single enormous eye with a mustard iris and a keyhole-shaped pupil, filling the keyhole of a vast door. Lashes cut from black paper strips, a soft cream lid that blinks (two-frame animation).

## Rigging checklist (every character)
Head, hair, hat/helmet, torso, upper arm L/R, forearm+hand L/R, upper leg L/R, lower leg+foot L/R, eyes (open, half, closed, wide), eyebrows (neutral, raised, worried, angry), mouth visemes (rest, A/I, E, O/U, M/B/P, F/V, L), one extra prop layer. Keep the same part names across characters so one rig template can be reused.
