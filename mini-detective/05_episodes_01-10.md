# Season 1: "The Keyhole Eye" — Episode Plots (Entries 1–10)

Each entry = one small case + one clue + one hook. The big mystery grows underneath.

---

## Entry 1 — The Button That Wasn't There
- **Hook:** In a city built from lost things, something just went missing.
- **Setup:** The Great Red Button, the pride of Button Square, has vanished overnight from its plinth.
- **Investigation:** No drag marks, no footprints. Juno mocks the idea that someone carried it off. Pip kneels at the plinth.
- **Clue:** A tiny symbol scratched into the stone: an eye shaped like a keyhole.
- **Turn:** Pip looks up. A single thread, taut, disappears into the dark above the plaza.
- **Cliffhanger:** The thread tightens. Something is still pulling.
- **Tags:** Introduces Pip, Juno, the Drift, the symbol.

## Entry 2 — Footprints on the Ceiling
- **Hook:** The thief left footprints. They were on the ceiling.
- **Setup:** Pip and Juno follow the thread to the top of the plaza wall. Inspector Brass arrives, declares "Drift accident. Case closed!"
- **Investigation:** Dusty prints walk across the underside of the shelf. Pip finds a strand of very fine silk with gold fibers.
- **Clue:** "Nobody in Pocket City makes thread this fine."
- **Turn:** Brass pats his coat. His badge is gone.
- **Cliffhanger:** On Brass's desk, in the dust where the badge lay: the keyhole eye.
- **Tags:** Brass introduced, second theft.

## Entry 3 — Madame Velvet's Silent Night
- **Hook:** The best singer in Pocket City stopped singing mid-note.
- **Setup:** At The Tin Lounge, Madame Velvet's music box is gone. She tells everyone it is "nothing."
- **Investigation:** Fuzz sells Pip a rumor for one crumb: Velvet received a letter with a keyhole-eye seal a week ago.
- **Clue:** The seal on the letter.
- **Turn:** Velvet admits she has heard of "the Keyhole Society", an old story about people who take what is lost, and what is loved.
- **Cliffhanger:** She hums the first notes of the music box tune. Pip freezes. He knows it, and he does not know why.
- **Tags:** Society named; Pip's mystery begins.

## Entry 4 — The Sock Marsh
- **Hook:** Nothing survives the Sock Marsh. Except the socks.
- **Setup:** Fuzz's tip leads Pip and Juno into the wool swamp. Comic hazards: sinking, mismatched sock "ghosts", Juno's glider stuck.
- **Investigation:** Following coins as stepping stones, they find a hidden warehouse in the fog.
- **Clue:** Rows of shelves sorted by color, each item tagged with a keyhole eye. A ledger page: "Collection 17 of 400."
- **Turn:** The Red Button is on a shelf, tagged "Lot 17".
- **Cliffhanger:** The warehouse lights go out from outside. Someone knows they are there.
- **Tags:** Scale of the plan; "Collection 17 of 400".

## Entry 5 — 3:11
- **Hook:** Every theft happened at exactly 3:11 in the morning.
- **Setup:** Pip realizes the thefts all match the minutes Professor Cog's great clock loses every night.
- **Investigation:** Pip stays up to watch the plaza. Time seems to skip. He finds an entry in his own notebook he does not remember writing: "Don't wait up. — P."
- **Clue:** Handwriting that is his, but the pencil is not his.
- **Turn:** Someone is borrowing the missing 11 minutes.
- **Cliffhanger:** At dawn Pip runs to Cog's workshop. Empty. The clock has stopped at 3:11.
- **Tags:** The 3:11 rule; Cog's disappearance.

## Entry 6 — Locked Room, Tiny Room
- **Hook:** A locked room. Inside a matchbox. From the inside.
- **Setup:** The workshop door is locked and bolted from within, yet Cog is gone.
- **Investigation:** Juno wants to break in. Pip studies the room's scale: the only way out is the keyhole, too small for Cog, but not for something thin.
- **Clue:** A line of tiny gears left on the floor spelling a message in gear code: "BRASS KNOWS".
- **Turn:** Cog left on purpose, down the pendulum shaft.
- **Cliffhanger:** Pip turns. Inspector Brass is in the doorway, holding a crumpled letter, trembling.
- **Tags:** Classic locked-room solved by scale.

## Entry 7 — Inspector Brass Has a Secret
- **Hook:** The man who closes every case just opened one of his own.
- **Setup:** Brass sits on a bench in Skirting Board Street and tells his story: forty years ago he lost something dear (his wife's ring, fallen through the Drift). A letter promised its return if he would "look away" from the thefts.
- **Investigation:** Pip does not scold him. Juno does, then stops.
- **Clue:** Brass hands Pip the Tube Key, the only key that can call out on the speaking tubes. "If you want answers, ask the one who writes the letters."
- **Turn:** Brass was never the thief. He was a prisoner of his own loss.
- **Cliffhanger:** The tube mouthpiece rattles by itself. A calm voice: "Hello, Detective."
- **Tags:** Brass redeemed; the Collector first heard.

## Entry 8 — The Voice in the Tube
- **Hook:** Pip had one chance to ask the thief a question.
- **Setup:** Back at the Bureau, Pip answers. The Collector is courteous and unhurried.
- **Investigation:** Pip asks three questions. The Collector answers one honestly and two with riddles.
- **Clue:** "Stop, and I will return everything you have ever lost. Even your name."
- **Turn:** Pip arrived in the Drift as a baby, wrapped in a handkerchief with a keyhole-eye stitched in the corner. The Collector knows who left him.
- **Cliffhanger:** Pip's hand shakes. "I'm going up there." Juno: "Nobody asked, but I'm coming."
- **Tags:** Pip's origin seed; stakes become personal.

## Entry 9 — The Night Delivery
- **Hook:** At 3:11 a.m., Pocket City looked up.
- **Setup:** Pip and Juno stake out the plaza. The thread drops from the ceiling, hooks a clock face, and pulls it up. They grab the thread and ride it.
- **Investigation:** Wind in the dark shaft. Juno's glider tears. They climb hand over hand.
- **Clue:** At the top, a hatch hinged with a tiny lock shaped like a keyhole eye. Pip's thimble fits the latch.
- **Turn:** The hatch opens onto light.
- **Cliffhanger:** They step into a place far bigger than Pocket City. Shelves rise out of sight.
- **Tags:** The thimble as key (seed); the Vault.

## Entry 10 — The Keyhole
- **Hook:** Pocket City was never the whole world.
- **Setup:** The Vault of Lost Things: towers of keys, umbrellas, gloves, letters, a child's shoe. At the far end, a door with a keyhole as tall as a building.
- **Investigation:** The Collector's voice echoes: "You are very close, Pip." Footprints in the dust lead to the keyhole.
- **Clue:** Through the keyhole, a light, and the thread running out toward it.
- **Turn:** Pip looks through. An enormous eye looks back.
- **Cliffhanger:** "Hello again, Pip." The eye blinks. Every light in Pocket City goes out. End card: "Season 1 ends. Entry 11 returns soon."
- **Tags:** Season finale; scale reveal.
