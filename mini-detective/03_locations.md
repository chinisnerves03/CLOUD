# Locations & Scene Design

**Master world rule:** Pocket City sits inside Dunmore's Lost & Found Office, behind the old radiator. Everything is built from lost human objects reused at 3 cm scale.

**Master background prompt (prepend to every location):**
```
Paper cut-out collage environment, flat construction-paper layers with torn edges and paper grain, built in separate foreground, midground and background layers for parallax animation, warm lamplight, limited palette: kraft brown #C89B6D, navy #1F2A44, mustard #E0A526, tomato red #C8452F, cream #F3E9D2, teal #2F8F8B, soft drop shadows between layers, no gradients, no text, vertical 9:16.
```

---

## 1. Button Square
- **What it is:** The central plaza. Round plaza paved with a huge cream shirt button; at the center a plinth that held the Great Red Button.
- **Mood:** Warm morning, golden light, crowd murmur.
- **Layers:** BG: skyline of thimble-roof houses and a pencil-stub clock tower. MG: the plinth, market stalls from matchboxes. FG: spools used as benches, thread banners.
- **Prompt add-on:**
  ```
  Location: a tiny plaza paved with a giant cream button, central stone plinth empty, market stalls made of matchboxes, houses with thimble roofs, spool benches, thread bunting, dawn light.
  ```
- **Used in:** Entries 1, 2.

## 2. Skirting Board Street
- **What it is:** The main road, running along the base of a human wall. Houses carved in the wood grain. Sky is the underside of the Office's shelf.
- **Mood:** Evening, amber street lamps from LED beads.
- **Prompt add-on:**
  ```
  Location: a long narrow street along a wooden skirting board, doors cut into wood grain, street lamps made from tiny beads, a bridge made of a paperclip, shelf underside as sky.
  ```
- **Used in:** Entries 2, 7.

## 3. The Detective Bureau
- **What it is:** Pip's office, inside an old pencil case. Zipper door. Desk from a domino. Wall map covered in red thread.
- **Mood:** Cozy, cluttered, rain sound from a dripping tap.
- **Prompt add-on:**
  ```
  Location: a tiny detective office inside an open pencil case, domino desk, wall map with red thread connecting pins, lamp made from a thimble and a bead, rain on a tiny window.
  ```
- **Used in:** Entries 1, 8, 10.

## 4. The Tin Lounge
- **What it is:** Madame Velvet's club inside a biscuit tin. Stage from a lid. Round booths from bottle caps.
- **Mood:** Blue smoke, red velvet, soft spotlight.
- **Prompt add-on:**
  ```
  Location: a tiny lounge inside a round biscuit tin, stage made from a lid, bottle-cap booths, red ribbon curtains, one spotlight from a torch, music-box on a stand.
  ```
- **Used in:** Entry 3.

## 5. The Sock Marsh
- **What it is:** A damp, soft, dangerous swamp of lost socks, mismatched and sinking, in the dark corner under the shelf. Everything is wool; every step sinks.
- **Mood:** Foggy, comic-creepy, mismatched colors.
- **Prompt add-on:**
  ```
  Location: a foggy swamp made of heaps of mismatched wool socks in many colors and patterns, sagging and sinking, lost-glove "reeds" sticking up, a narrow stepping-stone path made from coins.
  ```
- **Used in:** Entry 4.

## 6. Professor Cog's Workshop (and the Great Clock)
- **What it is:** A workshop inside a matchbox, attached to the base of the Great Clock (an old grandfather clock whose pendulum swings in the shaft above the city).
- **Mood:** Ticking, brass glints, gear shadows turning on the walls.
- **Prompt add-on:**
  ```
  Location: a tiny clockmaker workshop inside an open matchbox, shelves of gears, a workbench with tweezers and springs, a huge pendulum visible through a slot, wall of tiny clocks all showing different times.
  ```
- **Used in:** Entries 5, 6.

## 7. The Speaking Tubes
- **What it is:** Brass-colored tubes (straws, pen casings) connecting districts. Voices travel through them. The Collector speaks from the far end.
- **Mood:** Hollow echo, soft metallic hum.
- **Prompt add-on:**
  ```
  Location: a junction of tiny tubes made from straws and pen casings, brass funnel mouthpieces, labels in torn paper, shadows deep inside the tube openings.
  ```
- **Used in:** Entries 7, 8.

## 8. The Drift
- **What it is:** The gap behind the old radiator where lost things fall into Pocket City. A tall dark shaft with dust motes and floating coins.
- **Mood:** Quiet, soft falling items in slow motion.
- **Prompt add-on:**
  ```
  Location: a tall dark shaft behind a radiator, dust particles, coins, buttons and keys falling slowly, a faint thread running up through the darkness.
  ```
- **Used in:** Entries 1, 9.

## 9. The Vault of Lost Things
- **What it is:** Beyond the ceiling hatch: a hall far larger than Pocket City, towers of shelves of lost objects (keys, umbrellas, gloves, letters, a child's shoe). At the end, a door with a keyhole as tall as a building.
- **Mood:** Vast, silent, dusty gold light from the keyhole.
- **Prompt add-on:**
  ```
  Location: a vast hall with towering shelves of lost objects (keys, umbrellas, gloves, letters, single shoes), seen from far below, a colossal door at the end with a building-sized keyhole glowing mustard-yellow, tiny silhouettes at the bottom for scale.
  ```
- **Used in:** Entries 9, 10.

## Scale reminder
Whenever possible include a familiar object for scale (a coin, a paperclip, a thimble). Keep one human-scale object in the frame of any new place to remind viewers how small everything is.
