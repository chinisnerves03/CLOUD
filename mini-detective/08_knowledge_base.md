## 3. Secret canon (DRAFT — editable; never state before the planned season)

> Spoiler section. If you paste the primer into a model to write scripts, do not paste this section unless you want the model to plant clues.

1. **The Giant (Eye):** Mr. Alistair Dunmore, the human who owns the Lost & Found Office. Old, lonely, and the only human who knows Pocket City exists. He has watched it for forty years through a keyhole in his storeroom door. He never harmed it; he protected the Drift.
2. **The Keyhole Society:** Tiny followers of the Giant who believe he is a god. They send "offerings" (stolen items) up the thread because they think he asked for them. He did not.
3. **The Collector:** A Thimblefolk, the Society's leader, who intercepted a request meant for the Giant and twisted it for his own power. Polite, patient, and wrong.
4. **What the Giant actually lost:** His late wife's thimble. Pip's hat is that thimble. Pip arrived in the Drift wearing it.
5. **Pip's origin:** He is a foundling in the Drift, wrapped in a handkerchief with the Society's eye stitched by the Giant's wife, who thought the Drift might lead to a safe place. She was ill. This is why Pip knows Velvet's tune: it is the music box the wife played.
6. **Why 3:11:** The time the wife passed away.
7. **Season 2 question:** Will Pip give the thimble back and lose the only link to his past, or keep it and doom the Giant to loneliness?
